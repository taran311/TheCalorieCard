Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Who we are

The Calorie Card is a trading name of Hayer Software Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14026484), registered office Belmont Suite, Paragon Business Park, Chorley New Road, Horwich, Bolton, BL6 6HG. We're the "controller" of the personal data described here.

Questions or requests: support@thecaloriecard.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you choose, a display name and an avatar (a ready-made one, or a small copy of a photo you pick, which your friends can see). If you sign in with Google or Facebook we receive your name and email address (if your account has one) from them, and nothing else: we never post anything on your behalf.
  • Profile and goals: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, diet preferences and your calorie and macro targets.
  • What you log: foods, portions, calories and macros, recipes, direct debits, pots, closed days, weigh-ins and photos you choose to send for food recognition.
  • Social features: your friends list, friend requests, groups, messages, reactions, challenges, Guess the Calories games and high scores.
  • Games: your Games XP and level, your Daily Calorie Quiz guesses and score, Higher or Lower best score, and your Calorie Sense guesses. "Guess your own day" uses food you've already logged and doesn't store anything new.
  • Voice input: if you tap the microphone, your browser turns your speech into text using its own speech service (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari or Microsoft in Edge). We only receive the text, never the audio. If you don't use the microphone, nothing is recorded.
  • Gift codes: if you redeem a lifetime Premium gift code, we record which account used it and when, and show your display name to the person who gave you the code.
  • Coach: the messages you send to Calorie Coach and a summary of your day it needs to answer (for example calories left and foods logged today).
  • Payments: if you subscribe on our website, Stripe handles your card details; we only receive your subscription status, plan and a Stripe customer reference. If you subscribe in the Android app, Google Play handles the payment; we only receive your subscription status, plan and Google's purchase reference, which we check with Google. We never see your card number.
  • Technical: a notification token if you turn on reminders, game alerts or message notifications (which show the sender's name and the start of their message), and basic server logs (such as time and error messages) to keep the service running. We also count how often each game is played each day; these counts are just numbers, with no names or account details.

Information about your body, weight and eating can be health data, a special category under UK GDPR. We only process it because you ask us to (to work out and track your card), on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give when you enter it. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data or your account.

How we use it

  • To run your card: work out your daily budget, track what you log and show your history, streaks and statements (performance of our contract with you).
  • To look up foods and answer Coach questions using the services listed below (contract, and your consent for health data).
  • To show friends what you choose to share: friends can see your card, today's progress and the food you log, your Games XP and level, and your Daily Calorie Quiz score once they've played it too. Group members can see the same within a group. Don't add people you wouldn't want to see this.
  • To take payments and manage your subscription (contract).
  • To send reminders you've switched on, and essential emails such as verification and password resets (contract, or consent for reminders).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
  • To understand how the app is used, through simple daily totals (our legitimate interests). See "Usage statistics" below.

We don't sell your data, we don't show adverts, and we don't use your data to train AI models.

How long we keep it, and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (Profile → Account and privacy → Delete account) we delete your profile, food log, recipes, weigh-ins, messages, games and Games XP, take you out of friends lists, groups and challenges, cancel any subscription, remove your name from any gift code you redeemed, and delete your sign-in. Server logs are kept only for a short time. Stripe and Google Play keep payment records for as long as the law requires.

If you signed in with Facebook, deleting your account here deletes everything we got from Facebook too. You can also remove The Calorie Card from Facebook (Settings → Apps and websites), or email support@thecaloriecard.com and we'll delete your data for you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. You can download your food log and weigh-ins yourself from Profile → Account and privacy → Download my data, and correct most things directly in the app. For anything else, email us and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Cookies and storage

We only use storage that's needed for the app to work: keeping you signed in, remembering settings like dark mode, a gift link you've opened until you claim it, and similar. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Usage statistics

To see whether the app is working for people, our own server keeps daily totals: for example how many people opened the app, set up a card, logged their first food or came back the next day. These are counts only, not a record of who did what, and they're kept in our own database. We don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or any other third-party tracking, and no cookies are involved.

So each person is counted once, a few dates are stored on your account: when you set up your card, when you logged your first food, and when you last used the app (to within a few minutes). We use the last of these to see how the app is being used and to help if something goes wrong; only we can see it. They're all deleted with your account. The daily totals contain no personal data.

Food lookups and food data

When you look a food up, the words you typed are sent to our server, which works out the numbers. Answers are saved without anything that identifies you, so the next person to type the same food gets it instantly. If you fix the calories of a looked-up food, the food and your number are sent to our server so answers that several people correct get looked up again; we store a one-way code rather than your account, only to count how many different people corrected it.

Food data comes from FatSecret, Open Food Facts (food information from Open Food Facts contributors, made available under the Open Database Licence) and McCance and Widdowson's The Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0).

Children

The Calorie Card is for people aged 16 and over. It isn't designed for children, and calorie tracking isn't right for everyone. If we find out an account belongs to someone under 16, we'll delete it and its data. If you're worried about your relationship with food, please talk to your GP or contact Beat (beateatingdisorders.org.uk).

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
