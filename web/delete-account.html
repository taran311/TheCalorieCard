Delete your account

The Calorie Card, by Hayer Software Limited

In the app or on the website

  1. Open The Calorie Card (the Android app, or thecaloriecard.com) and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account and privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It happens straight away. If you'd like a copy of your food log and weigh-ins first, use Download my data on the same page.

Can't sign in?

Email support@thecaloriecard.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm it's you, delete everything and reply within one month (usually much sooner).

What's deleted

Your sign-in, profile and goals, food log, recipes, weigh-ins, messages, games and Games XP. You're taken out of friends lists, groups and challenges, any subscription is cancelled, and your name is removed from any gift code you redeemed.

What's kept: Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires, and server logs are kept only for a short time. Food lookups that were saved without anything identifying you stay, as they can't be linked back to you.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
